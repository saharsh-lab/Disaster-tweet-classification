Methodology

From raw text to dispatch

Normalization, sequence modeling and low-latency serverless inference — seven stages per signal.

  1. 01
    Signal ingestion
    Posts and field reports arrive through the REST API, the web console or the batch automation pipeline.
    Input: raw UTF-8 string (1–280 chars)
  2. 02
    Normalization
    URLs, mentions, hashtags and non-alphabetic noise are stripped; common stopwords are filtered.
    re.sub(r"http\S+|@\w+|[^a-z\s]", "", text.lower())
  3. 03
    Tokenization & padding
    Tokens map to IDs from a 5,000-word vocabulary; unknown words map to <OOV>. Sequences are post-padded to 30.
    int32 vector · shape (30,)
  4. 04
    Word embedding
    Each token becomes a 64-dimensional dense vector that captures relationships between crisis terms.
    tensor · shape (30, 64)
  5. 05
    Bidirectional LSTM
    The sequence is read forward and backward (64 units each); final hidden states are concatenated.
    hidden state · shape (128,)
  6. 06
    Dense head & sigmoid
    A 32-unit ReLU layer feeds a single sigmoid unit that outputs a probability between 0 and 1.
    p = 1 / (1 + e^−z) · threshold 0.50
  7. 07
    Resource extraction & dispatch
    When p ≥ 0.50 the signal is a request; a lexical analyzer tags the need — water, medical aid, shelter, food, clothing or rescue.
    Output: tagged telemetry record
